Risk limits

Applied before every order, whether it came from a strategy or from the terminal. Changes take effect on the next order; orders already with the broker are unaffected.

Set any limit to 0 to disable that check. Limits are held in memory — they revert to config.yaml on restart.

Blocks new entries once the day's P&L falls below this. Closing orders are always allowed — a loss limit must never trap you in a position.
