Backtest

Replays stored candles through the real strategy, broker, and risk code. Costs and one tick of adverse slippage are applied; positions still open at the end are force-closed so unrealized losses cannot hide.

Comma separated. The strategy subscribes to more as it runs — a straddle picks its option legs only after it sees a spot price.

A candle records four prices in no defined order. pessimist assumes the adverse extreme came first — when the data cannot say, a backtest should not assume the lucky ordering.
